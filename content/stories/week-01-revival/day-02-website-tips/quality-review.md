## Visual Quality — Critical Re-Review

Per an explicit owner directive, a technically-generated PNG is not
automatically publication-ready. Re-assessed against a professional
social-media design standard, not just legibility:

- **Typography:** plain — single weight/size hierarchy per line, no
  refined kerning/tracking, no accent treatment.
- **Hierarchy:** present (headline vs. supporting line) but expressed only
  through size, not through color, weight contrast, or layout structure.
- **Whitespace:** large empty margins above/below the text block, but this
  reads as default vertical-centering rather than an intentionally
  composed layout.
- **Composition / alignment:** centered text on a flat solid-color
  background — correct RTL alignment, but visually flat.
- **Visual interest:** minimal — no imagery, texture, iconography, or
  compositional device beyond text-on-color.
- **Consistency:** consistent across all 3 frames (a genuine strength),
  but it is the consistency of a plain template, not a designed system.
- **Human-design feel:** reads as a **wireframe/placeholder** for the
  message, not something an experienced social-media designer would ship
  as final client-facing output.
