# Design Direction — Website Tips Story (Day 2)

BRAND VISUAL STATUS: FLEXIBLE — FINAL IDENTITY PENDING
(`brand/visual-identity.md`)

## Visual System
- Background: solid neutral dark navy (`#0f1b2d`) — a temporary,
  non-brand-locked neutral chosen for legibility and calm professionalism,
  not a final brand color.
- Typography: system Persian-capable sans-serif, white text, large size
  (~64px at 1080×1920), centered, generous line-height for RTL readability.
- No logo, no current brand color treated as permanent.
- Consistent background/type system across all 3 frames for sequence
  cohesion.

## Layout
Full-bleed 9:16 (1080×1920), text vertically centered, ~80px horizontal
margins. Frame 3 reserves lower third empty for the native Question Box
sticker Instagram adds at posting time.

## Text Placement
Centered block, right-to-left reading order.

## What Must NOT Appear
- Any specific brand color/logo treated as final
- Stock imagery
- Overcrowded text (kept to 2 short lines per frame)
