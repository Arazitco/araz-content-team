# Design Direction — Team/Human Introduction Reel (Day 6)

BRAND VISUAL STATUS: FLEXIBLE — FINAL IDENTITY PENDING

## Approach
Minimal graphic treatment by design — authenticity, not visual polish, is
the point of this format (per `strategy/content-strategy.md` Format
Strategy on human/expert content). Avoid over-designing this Reel into
feeling corporate/scripted.

## Layout
9:16 vertical, subject centered or slightly off-center (rule-of-thirds),
natural indoor lighting preferred over studio lighting.

## Typography
Minimal on-screen text — only Scenes 1, 3, 4, 5 per `storyboard.md`; same
Persian-capable sans-serif system as other Week 1 assets, but used
sparingly here.

## Cover Direction
Cover frame: Scene 1 (hook moment), a genuine, approachable expression —
not a posed "corporate headshot" look.

## What Must NOT Appear
- Any AI-generated or synthetic talking-head
- Scripted-sounding, over-produced delivery
- Any current logo/color/font treated as permanent
