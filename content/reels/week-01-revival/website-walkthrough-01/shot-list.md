## Preferred Approach: Screenshot Animation, Not New Screen Recording
The 3 supplied assets are **static screenshots**, not video. Rather than
requesting a fresh screen recording (extra owner effort), the intended
approach is to **animate the real screenshots** — slow vertical pans and
gentle zooms across the actual UI — which is a standard, legitimate
technique real motion designers use when working from stills. This keeps
the real interface as the hero visual without inventing footage.
