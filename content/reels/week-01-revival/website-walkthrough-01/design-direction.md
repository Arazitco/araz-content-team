# Design Direction — Carlin Ladies Website Walkthrough Reel (Day 1)

BRAND VISUAL STATUS: FLEXIBLE — FINAL IDENTITY PENDING
(`brand/visual-identity.md`)

## Guiding Principle
The real Carlin Ladies interface is the hero visual. Every frame is a real
screenshot (animated via pan/zoom), never obscured or replaced by a
generic AI-generated graphic, fake browser chrome, or invented UI.

## Layout
9:16 full-bleed. Screenshots are cropped/framed intentionally per scene
(see `storyboard.md`) rather than shrunk to fit with letterboxing. On-
screen text sits in a reserved safe zone (top or bottom third, whichever
doesn't cover key UI in that specific screenshot) with a subtle gradient
scrim for legibility — never a solid box that hides the interface.

## Typography
Modern, minimal, highly readable Persian-capable sans-serif (per
`brand/visual-identity.md`), consistent with other Week 1 assets. Short
lines only (~6 words), since the real screenshot needs to stay legible
and central.

## Motion Direction
Slow, controlled pans/zooms (max ~8% scale change, per `shot-list.md`) —
enough to feel alive without looking like a jittery or over-produced
effect. No spins, glitches, or flashy transitions.

## Cropping Direction
- Scene 1: full hero image, no crop.
- Scene 3: crop tight enough to read the price and size selector clearly.
- Scene 4: crop directly to the cross-sell block — this is the "hero
  detail" of the whole Reel.
- Scene 5: crop to the filter sidebar specifically, not the whole page.

## Cover Direction
**Headline:** «این سایت فروشگاهی چطور کمک می‌کنه یه ست کامل بخرید؟»
**Cover frame:** the homepage hero (Scene 1), text in the lower third.

## What Must NOT Appear
- Generic AI-generated graphics, 3D objects, glow/neon effects, or
  meaningless gradients
- Fake browser chrome or a fabricated UI not present in the real
  screenshots
- Any current logo/color/font treated as final ArasSystem branding
- Excessive glassmorphism or cheap-template visual clutter
