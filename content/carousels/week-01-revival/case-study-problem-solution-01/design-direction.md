# Design Direction — Mahtaj Skinland Design Breakdown Carousel (Day 4)

BRAND VISUAL STATUS: FLEXIBLE — FINAL IDENTITY PENDING

## Layout
Consistent 4:5 frame across all 8 slides. Slides 1–7 use the real
screenshot (cropped per slide, see `structure.md`) as full-bleed
background with a bottom or top gradient scrim for text; Slide 8 is a
text-only end card using the same neutral background system as other
Week 1 text slides.

## Typography
Same Persian-capable sans-serif system as the rest of Week 1. One
headline + at most one supporting line per slide.

## Visual Hierarchy
One observation per slide — never combine two unrelated design
observations on the same slide.

## Cropping Direction
Detailed per-slide in `structure.md`; general rule: crop tight enough
that the specific element being discussed (category grid, brand section,
carousel, about section) is clearly readable, not a full-page screenshot
shrunk down.

## What Must NOT Appear
- A fabricated "before" version of the site
- Any claim about why a design choice was made
- Generic AI graphics, 3D objects, neon/glow effects, meaningless
  gradients, fake browser UI, or cheap-template visual clutter
- Permanent brand color/logo treated as final
