Source Type: Public Instagram observation
Status: HISTORICAL BASELINE

## Evidence Classification

Verified public observations:
Facts directly observed from the Instagram account and recorded in this report.

Initial strategic hypotheses:
Interpretations or testing ideas derived from those observations.

Unavailable/private metrics:
Metrics such as complete Reach, Saves, Shares, Profile Visits, Watch Time, Retention, Link Clicks, and full Story Insights remain unknown until verified Instagram Insights access is available.

## Strategic Use Rule

This baseline is evidence, not a permanent strategy.

It MUST NOT:

- lock future strategy to historical formats
- force repetition of old topics
- override the current 70% Website Design / 30% SEO default
- limit experimentation
- be treated as proof that historical winners will win again
- prevent the strategy from evolving

It MAY be used to:

- understand account history
- identify historical patterns
- identify overused or underused formats
- generate hypotheses
- identify content worth retesting
- compare future revival performance against the historical baseline

New verified Instagram Insights and newly published content performance must progressively receive greater strategic weight than historical public observations.

If newer verified Insights conflict with historical observations, prefer the newer verified data.

Important:

The observed post with 5,492 likes and 132 comments is a significant historical outlier worth investigating, NOT a format that must be copied.

The historically high volume of SEO content does NOT override the current strategic priority:

70% Website Design
