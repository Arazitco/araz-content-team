## Reporting Periods Available

Instagram's native date-range selector on the Account Insights and Content Insights pages offers exactly four options:

- Last 7 days
- Last 14 days
- Last 30 days
- Last 90 days

**No longer native historical range exists in this UI** (no "last 6 months" / "last year" / "lifetime" option). This is a platform limitation, not a retrieval failure.

Because the account has been inactive at the post/reel/story-publishing level for longer than 90 days, the following data was collected instead as the "most recent period with meaningful activity":

- **Individual per-item insights** were pulled directly from the 6 most recent actual content items (5 Reels + 1 sampled archived Story) by opening each one and using its own "View insights" panel. These panels are not bound by the 7/14/30/90-day selector — they show lifetime-to-date performance for that specific item.
- The account's **Story Archive** ("View archive" → Stories) was checked and confirmed to hold content further back than 90 days; the most recent archived Story is dated **20 Feb 2026** (~27 weeks before data collection). Older archived Stories exist further back (5 Jan 2026, 27 Dec 2025, 21 Dec 2025, and more not fully enumerated).

No data was invented for periods Instagram does not expose. Where a longer/lifetime aggregate simply is not offered by the UI, this is marked N/A below rather than estimated.

## Data Limitations

- UNKNOWN / N/A fields are marked exactly as such — never converted to 0.
- **VERIFIED PRIVATE INSIGHT** vs. **INTERPRETATION** vs. **UNKNOWN** labels are used throughout per the evidence rule; the two categories are never mixed in one line.
- Account-level "Views/Interactions by content type" figures for the 30- and 90-day windows include residual activity (views/likes/etc. arriving during that window) on content published **before** that window — see the "Important Observed Patterns" section. This is an INTERPRETATION, not something Instagram states explicitly in its UI.
- Instagram's native "Top content" ranking tool (Content Insights → sort by Accounts engaged / Interactions / Shares / Viewers / Views) only ranks content **published within the selected date window**. Since zero content was published in the last 90 days, this tool returned "No content available" for every filter and metric combination tested (All / Posts / Reels / Stories). Consequently, the "Top Performing Content" section below is **manually compiled** from the individually-opened item insights, not an Instagram-generated ranking — this is explicitly flagged wherever it applies.
- Per-item Reels insights panels do not expose **Plays, Watch time, Average watch time, Retention, or Completion rate** for this account in the current UI — every such field is N/A, not estimated.
- Per-item Story insights do not expose a **"Back" navigation metric** (only Forward, Exited, Next story are shown under "Navigation") — marked N/A.
- **Audience demographics (age, gender, location)** did not appear anywhere on the Account Insights page for either the 30-day or 90-day window — marked N/A. "Most active times" (a followers-online-by-hour chart) is available and is reported below, but it is not scoped to the selected date range in an obvious way (values were unchanged between the 30-day and 90-day views), so it is best read as the account's current standing audience-activity pattern rather than a period-specific metric.
- **Follower growth (net gained/lost) as a distinct delta metric** was not found anywhere in the Account Insights UI — only a static "Total followers" snapshot (4,652, identical across both the 30- and 90-day views) is shown. Marked N/A.
- **Unfollows** as a distinct count: N/A — not exposed.

## Important Observed Patterns

Per instruction, ACCOUNT INACTIVITY EFFECT and CONTENT PERFORMANCE SIGNALS are kept separate below — poor recent totals are not read as evidence of weak content strategy.

**ACCOUNT INACTIVITY EFFECT (VERIFIED PRIVATE INSIGHT):**
- Zero new Posts, Reels, or Stories were published in the last 90 days.
- The low absolute totals for 30-day and 90-day Views (524 / 2,084) and Interactions (3 / 6) reflect an account that has been dormant at the publishing level, not necessarily weak content — these numbers are residual traffic on older content, not a live-campaign baseline.
- The Story Archive shows the account was still active as recently as ~20 Feb 2026 (Stories), meaning the true "last active" date is more recent than the last Reel (8 Jan 2026) — worth confirming directly with the account owner if precision matters for the revival plan.

**CONTENT PERFORMANCE SIGNALS (from the 6 individually-sampled recent items — VERIFIED PRIVATE INSIGHT, small sample, not a statistically representative set):**
- The single best-performing sampled item by every engagement metric (Likes, Comments, Shares, Accounts engaged) is the internal team/behind-the-scenes catering event Reel (Oct 26, 2025) — a non-promotional, non-sales piece of content.
- The Black Friday "5 steps" Reel reached by far the most non-followers (91.8% of its 2,314 views) — a strong discovery/reach signal — but converted that reach into comparatively modest engagement (27 likes, 3 comments) relative to its view count.
- The 3 promotional/sales-oriented Reels (Yalda AI promo, both Black Friday reels) each generated exactly 1 follow and 1 profile-activity event; the purely artistic calligraphy Reel generated 0 of either.
- INTERPRETATION: The Black Friday "5 steps" Reel showed an unusually high non-follower view share (91.8%), while the team/behind-the-scenes Reel showed stronger engagement depth in this small sample. Both are testing signals, not general conclusions.
