# ArasSystem Brand Voice

Operational voice guide for anyone or any agent producing ArasSystem content.
Full brand positioning lives in `knowledge/positioning.md`; this file covers
tone and language only.

## Brand Personality
- Calm
- Precise
- Professional
- Human
- Responsible
- Trustworthy
- Confident without arrogance
- Friendly without becoming overly casual
- Modern without chasing trends

## Brand Archetypes
- The Sage
- The Caregiver

## Voice Traits
- Clear and understandable
- Calm and confident
- Professional but human
- Honest
- Responsible
- Consultative, not aggressively sales-driven
- Helpful
- No unnecessary technical jargon

## Writing Principles
- Lead with education, clarification, and helping the reader make a better
  decision — not with a hard sell.
- Every piece of content should build trust, responsibility, sustainable
  growth, or transparency.
- Write like a knowledgeable, responsible partner speaking to a business
  owner — not like an ad.
- Be specific and useful; avoid vague claims that could apply to any company.
- Content must feel human and professional. It must not read as generic
  AI-generated copy.

## Preferred Language / Concepts
Use and reinforce these ideas where natural:
- Trust
- Growth
- Partnership
- Transparency
- Responsibility
- Quality
- Solution
- Cooperation
- Improvement

## Language to Avoid
Never use:
- "Guaranteed #1 ranking"
- "Explosion in sales"
- "Miracle"
- "Cheapest"
- "Best in Iran"
- "Unbeatable"
- "Guaranteed"

Also avoid:
- Unrealistic promises
- Fear-based selling
- Attacking competitors
- Exaggerated marketing language
- Generic AI-style phrasing, filler, and robotic tone

## Social Media Tone
- Calm, confident, and consultative — like trusted advice from a partner who
  understands the business, not a salesperson pushing a deal.
- Keep language plain and jargon-free; explain technical ideas simply.
- Stay warm and human without becoming casual, jokey, or overly informal.
- Never promise guaranteed results, instant transformation, or the cheapest
  price.
- Website Design is the primary content focus (70% default); SEO is
  secondary (30% default) — see `knowledge/company.md`.

## Pre-Publish Voice Checklist
Before any post, reel, carousel, story, or caption is approved, confirm:

- [ ] Does this sound human and professional, not generic or AI-like?
- [ ] Is the tone calm and confident, without arrogance or hype?
- [ ] Have I avoided all "Language to Avoid" words/phrases above?
- [ ] Does the content educate, clarify, or help decision-making — not just sell?
- [ ] Does it reflect trust, responsibility, transparency, or sustainable growth?
- [ ] Have I avoided fear-based selling and competitor attacks?
- [ ] Are all claims specific and true — no invented stats, results, or guarantees?
- [ ] Has the owner given explicit approval before publishing?
