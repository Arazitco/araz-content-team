# ArasSystem Visual Identity

## Status: PENDING REDESIGN

The ArasSystem website and visual identity are currently being redesigned.
Do NOT treat any current logo, exact color codes, fonts, or social templates
as permanently approved. Nothing visual should be locked into content
production based on temporary/current assets.

## Approved Visual Principles
These principles are approved and should guide visual direction regardless
of the pending redesign outcome:

- Trust
- Clarity
- Simplicity
- Precision
- Consistency
- Calmness
- Timelessness
- Professional quality
- Minimalism
- Adequate whitespace
- Clear hierarchy
- Readable typography
- Real, high-quality imagery rather than cliché stock imagery
- Motion only when purposeful

## Color Direction (broad, not final)
- Blue direction for trust/stability
- Orange as a limited accent
- Neutral colors for most structure
