# ArasSystem Audience

Only use confirmed information.

## Geographic Market
All of Iran.

## Audience Segments
- Companies
- E-commerce businesses / online shops
- Service businesses
- Medium-sized businesses
- Growing brands
- Trading companies / B2B businesses
- Manufacturers / product businesses
- More broadly, businesses that want a professional online presence and want to look and operate more professionally online

## Ideal Customer Mindset
- Long-term view
- Values quality
- Values responsibility and support
- Does not choose solely based on lowest price
- Wants a reliable team rather than multiple disconnected vendors

## Who ArasSystem Is NOT Targeting
- Customers whose primary goal is the cheapest website
- Buyers who only want the lowest price
- Buyers who sacrifice quality for speed
- Buyers who want shortcuts or unrealistic results
- Buyers who expect guaranteed or overnight transformation

## Main Pain Points / Concerns
- Will the website really help the business?
- Who will be responsible if technical problems happen?
- Will the provider remain after delivery?
- Is the investment worthwhile?
- Does the provider understand the business or only want to sell a service?
