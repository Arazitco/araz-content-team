# ArasSystem Instagram Revival 30-Day Plan — V1

Strategic, non-final plan built directly from
`reports/instagram-revival-audit-v1.md`. This is a **strategic skeleton**,
not final content: no captions, Reel scripts, or Story copy are final.
Every item still passes through Content Production → Creative Director →
Quality Editor → Owner Review → Explicit Approval → Publisher before
anything is published (see "Owner Approval" below).

## Default Content Mix

70% Website Design / 30% SEO governs the subject focus of **service-related
feed content** (see `strategy/content-strategy.md`). Cross-cutting content
(trust, social proof, team, brand, FAQ, behind-the-scenes) supports both
categories and does not create a third service category, so it is excluded
from the ratio denominator below.

Of the **13 total planned Feed items**, 11 are core service-related items
(8 Website Design / 3 SEO) and 2 are Cross-cutting/supporting items. The
service-topic mix is calculated only across those 11 service-related
items — never across all 13:

- Website Design: 8 / 11 ≈ 73%
- SEO: 3 / 11 ≈ 27%

This approximates the default 70/30 within a small monthly sample; it is
not a permanent ratio change.

## Cadence Principle

Quality over volume. This plan does **not** assume one Feed post per day —
13 Feed publishing days are planned across 30 days, supplemented by
flexible daily Stories, preparation days, review days, and at least one
deliberate no-production day. Story frequency varies by day (1–4
stories), never mechanically fixed.

## Revival Phases

**PHASE 1 — Reactivation (Days 1–10):** Consistency, brand re-entry,
audience warming, portfolio and human-content introduction.

**PHASE 2 — Testing (Days 11–21):** Test formats, hooks, human/expert
video, portfolio styles, Story interactions, website education.

**PHASE 3 — Optimization (Days 22–30):** Increase promising formats
(pending real data), reduce weak signals, keep experimenting. No numeric
targets are set in advance — see the Weekly Review Checkpoints for how
this gets decided with real data.

## Portfolio Requirement Coverage

Portfolio content appears in multiple distinct forms across the month, not
repeated mechanically:
- Completed project website walkthrough Reel (Day 1)
- Carousel case study, client problem → design solution (Day 4)
- Before/After static + Reel reveal (Days 8, 10)
- Work-in-progress / industry-specific project (Days 16–17)
- Mobile UX showcase (Day 19)
- Case study wrap combining portfolio + human expertise (Day 29)

Each explains what was built, why it was designed that way, and what
problem it addresses. No business results are claimed without evidence.

## Human Content Test Coverage

- Day 6: Team talking-to-camera, introducing the revival (TEST 02)
- Day 15: Expert opinion/commentary on an SEO decision (TEST 02 variant)
- Day 26: Team/behind-the-scenes retest (TEST 02 retest)

These are experiments per the private-Insights signal (Section 5 of the
audit), not assumed winners.

Story purposes rotate across Relationship, Education, Interaction,
Portfolio, Social proof, WIP, FAQ, Team, Traffic, Consultation, and
Feedback collection — never the same pattern daily. Volume ranges from 0
(Days 11, 30) to 4 (Days 4, 10, 17), matching what each day's content
actually supports.

SEO appears 3 times this month (Days 12, 15, 24) — website+SEO connection,
an expert-commentary angle, and common mistakes. None imply specific
packages, deliverables, or guarantees beyond what's confirmed in

## Optional Website Articles

No article repurposing is forced into this plan. If a current, relevant
article exists in `articles/` when a given day's content is produced,
`content-planner`/`content-writer` may repurpose it (Article → Reel /
Carousel / Stories / Website CTA) — otherwise the day proceeds as planned
without one.

## CTA Strategy Applied

question, Quiz/Vote, View portfolio, WhatsApp/Request consultation, No
CTA). WhatsApp/consultation CTAs appear only on Days 20, 22, and 29 — not
on every post or story.

## Experiment Design

**TEST 01 — Website Walkthrough Reel**
- Hypothesis: A real walkthrough of a completed website builds more trust
  and saves than a static portfolio image.
- Variable being tested: format (walkthrough Reel vs. static portfolio
  image), holding topic (completed project) constant.
- Primary metric: Saves; secondary: watch-related signals if exposed.
- When to review: Week 1 checkpoint (Day 7), then Day 19 variant, final
  read at End-of-Month Review.

**TEST 02 — Talking-Head / Expert Content**
- Hypothesis: Human/expert-delivered content increases engagement depth
  relative to graphic-only content, consistent with the private-Insights
  team-Reel signal.
- Variable being tested: human/talking-head presence vs. graphic-only
  delivery, holding general topic area flexible across the three
  instances (revival intro, SEO opinion, team retest) to sample the
  format broadly rather than one exact topic.
- Primary metric: Comments and accounts engaged.
- When to review: Week 1 checkpoint (Day 7) for the first instance,
  full comparison at End-of-Month Review across all three instances.

**TEST 03 — Portfolio Before/After**
- Hypothesis: Before/After framing communicates redesign value more
  clearly than a single after-only showcase.
- Variable being tested: Before/After framing vs. after-only, holding
  format (static then Reel) as two separate sub-tests.
- Primary metric: Saves and shares.
- When to review: Week 2 checkpoint (Day 14).

**TEST 06 — Website Design + Growth Angle**
- Hypothesis: Framing website design explicitly within the Website →
  Trust → UX → Conversion → Leads → Growth chain increases perceived
  authority and profile visits versus a design-only framing.
- Variable being tested: growth-chain framing vs. a design-only framing
  (compared conceptually against earlier portfolio posts, not a strict
  A/B).
- Primary metric: Profile visits and follows.
- When to review: Week 4 checkpoint (Day 28).

Only one variable is tested per experiment; unrelated variables (visual
identity, posting time, etc.) remain uncontrolled/constant where possible
given real-world constraints.

## End-of-Month Review

`instagram-analyst` produces the Monthly Performance Report using
`reports/monthly-performance-template.md`, covering: cadence, formats,
portfolio share, Story strategy, Website Design vs SEO performance,
traffic, and lead signals — built from the four weekly reports plus any
newly available account-level data.

`growth-strategist` then reviews the report and, together with
`content-planner`, recommends: KEEP / INCREASE / REDUCE / STOP / TEST NEXT
MONTH for the next cycle.

**Any change to the 70% Website Design / 30% SEO default remains OWNER
APPROVAL ONLY** — the Strategist may recommend it with evidence, but may
not apply it.
