# Template: Question Box Story Frame (V1)

## When To Use
A Story frame inviting followers to ask a question, with an Instagram
"Question" sticker to be placed by the owner in the Instagram app itself.

## 1. Content Structure
One inviting headline + optional one supporting line naming what kinds of
questions are welcome. Never draw or render a fake question-box graphic
— that's a real Instagram sticker added after export, not part of the
image file.

## 2. Visual Structure
- Full-bleed 9:16 background — neutral/brand-flexible.
- Centered text in the upper half, leaving clean, genuinely empty space
  in the lower third/center for the real Instagram sticker to sit once
  added in-app.
- No other visual clutter competing with the sticker placement zone.

## 3. Production Rules
- Output: 1080×1920 (9:16).
- Typography: per `templates/production/typography-system.md`.
- Route: **Route B (ChatGPT Image)** or **Route C (Canva)** — no
  real-screenshot dependency.
- Export: PNG, named per `delivery/file-naming-convention.md`.
- Note in the delivery manifest that the Question sticker itself is
  added by the owner in Instagram, not part of the exported file.
