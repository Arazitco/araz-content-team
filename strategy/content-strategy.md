# ArasSystem Content Strategy (V1)

Operational strategy for the ArasSystem Instagram account. Business facts
live in `knowledge/*.md`; brand tone lives in `brand/voice.md`; this file
defines *how* content decisions should be made. Used primarily by
`growth-strategist`, `content-planner`, `content-manager`,
`instagram-analyst`, `story-producer`, and `portfolio-producer`.

## Strategic Objective

Instagram's role for ArasSystem is to revive an inactive professional
account into a trust-building, authority-building channel that showcases
real website work and moves qualified audiences toward contact,
consultation, or WhatsApp conversation — not to chase likes or views for
their own sake.

## Business Outcomes (Hierarchy)

```
Attention → Reach → Trust → Authority → Profile Visit →
Website / WhatsApp / Consultation → Qualified Lead
```

Every content decision should be traceable to a step in this chain.
Vanity metrics (likes/views alone) are signals, not goals.

## Default Service Mix

**70% Website Design / 30% SEO** — this is the default, not a hard limit.

- This ratio governs the primary subject focus of feed content over time,
  not every individual Story or brand/human post.
- Trust, team, social proof, and brand content may support both categories
  without becoming a third competing service category.
- Only the owner may permanently change this ratio. `growth-strategist` may
  recommend a change based on evidence, but must flag it explicitly for
  owner approval rather than applying it.

## Revival Strategy

The account returns from inactivity. Approach in phases — none of these
phases carry invented performance targets; each defines what to learn.

1. **Reactivation** — Resume consistent, quality-first publishing. Reintroduce
   the account's focus (Website Design primary, SEO secondary) and
   reestablish visual/tonal consistency after years of varied templates.
   Learn: does the audience still respond, and to what.
2. **Learning / Testing** — Deliberately test format hypotheses (see Format
   Strategy below) and topic pillars against real Instagram Insights once
   connected. Learn: which formats, hooks, and pillars earn reach, saves,
   and trust signals for *this* audience now — not from historical data
   alone.
3. **Optimization / Scaling** — Double down on what the Learning phase
   confirms; retire what underperforms; scale cadence/format investment
   accordingly. Learn: what a sustainable, high-quality weekly rhythm looks
   like.

## Content Goal Framework

Each piece of feed content should have exactly **one primary goal**:

- **REACH** — extend visibility to non-followers
- **TRUST** — build credibility and confidence in ArasSystem
- **AUTHORITY** — demonstrate expertise
- **EDUCATION** — help the audience understand or decide
- **ENGAGEMENT** — invite comments/shares/saves through genuine value
- **PORTFOLIO PROOF** — show real completed or in-progress work
- **WEBSITE TRAFFIC** — drive clicks to arazitco.com
- **LEAD GENERATION** — prompt contact, consultation, or WhatsApp

## Format Strategy

- **Reels** — real, human/expert-delivered video is a **test opportunity**:
  historically underused relative to static graphics, not a proven winner.
  Test conversational/expert formats (the observed podcast-style outlier —
  "درآمد نجومی سایت خانمی", 5,492 likes / 132 comments — is a hypothesis to
  test, not evidence of a formula, since full account engagement data isn't
  available).
- **Carousels** — strong fit for education and portfolio walkthroughs.
- **Static posts** — historically the dominant format; keep using them
  where they suit the message, but do not default to them purely out of
  habit.
- **Stories** — daily relationship-building and admin support (see Story
  Strategy).
- **Portfolio/case studies** — core pillar (see Portfolio Strategy).
- **Behind-the-scenes / human/team content** — supports trust and brand
  personality; used selectively, not as filler.
- **Educational content / expert commentary** — supports authority; must
  stay specific and useful, not generic.

## Portfolio Strategy

Portfolio is a **core pillar**, not a supporting category.

- **A. Work-in-progress content** — behind the scenes, UI previews,
  before/after, design decisions, coming soon.
- **B. Completed-project content** — Reel, Carousel, static showcase, Story
  sequence, Before/After, case study, website link, CTA.

Portfolio content should explain not only *what* was designed, but *why*
decisions were made, *what business/user problem* existed, and *what
solution* was implemented. Never fabricate client results or metrics that
weren't provided.

## Story Strategy

Stories function as daily relationship-building and admin support,
reducing the workload a dedicated admin would otherwise carry.

Categories: education, interaction (poll/quiz/question), portfolio, work
in progress, social proof, FAQ, website tips, SEO tips, team/behind the
scenes, trend response, consultation CTA, website/link traffic.

Do not mechanically require the same number or pattern of Stories every
day — `content-planner`/`story-producer` should vary sequences by goal and
available material.

## Social Proof Strategy

Use existing strengths: customer testimonials, the "اعتماد شما" trust
highlight, portfolio projects, project experience, and ArasSystem's
business history. Do not overuse testimonial content or let the account
read as continuous advertising — social proof supports other pillars, it
is not a standalone content flood.

## Website Article Repurposing

Website articles are **optional**, used only when the topic aligns with
current strategy, is useful/current, supports audience value, and creates
a meaningful website-traffic opportunity.

Possible repurposing chain: Article → Reel → Carousel → Stories → FAQ →
Website CTA. Never force article usage into a calendar.

## CTA Strategy

Confirmed CTA directions: Contact, Consultation, WhatsApp, View portfolio,
Visit website, Request information (see `knowledge/offers.md`).

CTAs should vary by content intent — do not attach aggressive sales CTAs
to every educational post.

## Content Originality Strategy

Competitor research (`research-intelligence`) may inform patterns, content
gaps, topic opportunities, format trends, and hook mechanisms — never
content to copy or lightly rewrite. Before producing similar topics, check
`archive/` where practical to reduce unnecessary repetition of ArasSystem's
own past content (avoid recreating large volumes of generic SEO cards,
repetitive static education, repost-style clusters, or disconnected
seasonal promotions, as seen historically).

## Weekly Learning Loop

```
Published content → instagram-analyst → Performance Report →
growth-strategist → content-planner → Next Week Calendar
```

Each weekly report should produce: KEEP / INCREASE / REDUCE / STOP / TEST
NEXT WEEK recommendations, feeding directly into the next calendar.

## Monthly Strategy Review

Review: top formats, top topics, portfolio performance, Website Design vs
SEO performance, Story signals, audience response, website traffic where
available, lead signals, competitor/trend changes.

The permanent 70/30 mix is never changed automatically. `growth-strategist`
may recommend a change; only the owner approves a strategic override.

## Measurement Framework

Map metrics to content goals — **use these only once Instagram Insights
data becomes accessible**; do not assume current availability:

- **Reach content**: reach, non-follower reach, plays/views
- **Engagement/education**: saves, shares, comments
- **Authority/trust**: profile visits, follows, meaningful comments/replies
- **Traffic**: link clicks, website sessions (where available)
- **Lead**: WhatsApp/contact/consultation signals (where trackable)
- **Stories**: views, completion, replies, sticker interactions, link
  clicks, exits

The historical baseline (see below) contains verified public observations;
strategic conclusions derived from those observations should be treated as
hypotheses until tested against new content performance and verified
Instagram Insights, once connected — the authoritative performance
source.

## Instagram Baseline

Full source: `analytics/baseline/arazitco-instagram-public-audit-2026-08-28.md`.
That report contains **verified public observations** — facts directly
observed from the account. This section summarizes them and classifies
strategic use per the same three-tier distinction as the source report:

- **Verified public observations** — facts directly observed and recorded
  in the baseline report (see below).
- **Initial strategic hypotheses** — interpretations or testing ideas
  derived from those observations; treated as hypotheses, not conclusions,
  until tested against new content performance and verified Instagram
  Insights.
- **Unknown until Instagram Insights is connected** — private metrics the
  public report could not capture; never fabricated or estimated.

**Verified public observations:** @arazitco — 371 posts, 4,652 followers,
285 following, existing link to arazitco.com. Historical content
categories observed: website portfolio/client projects, SEO/digital-
marketing education, relatable business/team humor, Threads repost-style
content, seasonal promotions, tech/business news, internal brand/team/
milestone content. 43 Story Highlights exist, with strong portfolio-by-
industry categorization and a dedicated trust/testimonial highlight
("اعتماد شما"). A sampled engagement outlier was observed: a podcast-style
Reel ("درآمد نجومی سایت خانمی") with 5,492 likes / 132 comments.

**Initial strategic hypotheses derived from these observations:**
- The podcast-style outlier suggests conversational/expert/human formats
  deserve testing — not that it is proven to be the account's best post,
  since full engagement data for all 371 posts wasn't captured.
- Static educational graphics and SEO content have historically dominated
  volume, while real-video Reels and visually consistent branding have
  been comparatively rare (7–8+ visual eras observed); both are test
  opportunities for V1, not proven strategies to repeat or avoid.

**Unknown until Instagram Insights is connected:** full reach, saves,
shares, profile visits, follows generated, link clicks, watch time,
retention, and complete Story/post-level engagement data for all 371
posts. These must never be fabricated or estimated.

## Visual Identity Note

Visual identity is **PENDING REDESIGN**. This strategy does not depend on
any current logo, color, font, or template. See
`brand/visual-identity.md` for approved visual principles; `creative-director`
applies flexible, non-hard-coded direction until final assets are approved.

## Quality & Originality Requirements

All content must be human-like, professional, specific, useful, original,
natural in Persian, non-generic, non-robotic, and aligned with
`brand/voice.md`. Avoid generic AI copy, repetitive content, competitor
copying, empty viral content, exaggerated claims, excessive promotion, and
constant direct selling. Content should help audiences understand, learn,
decide, evaluate website/design decisions, recognize business
risks/opportunities, trust ArasSystem, and see proof of its work.

## Owner Approval

No post, reel, carousel, story, or scheduled publication may be published
without explicit, current owner approval — enforced by `quality-editor`
(quality gate) and `publisher` (publishing gate). See `CLAUDE.md`.
