## 1. Executive Summary

ArasSystem is a 12+ year business (`knowledge/company.md`) whose @arazitco
Instagram account — previously active, with a historical 9-year
anniversary milestone post observed in the public baseline — carries 371
posts, 4,652 followers, and 285 following, but has published no new Posts,
Reels, or Stories in the last 90 days — the most recent archived Story is
dated 20 Feb 2026. The account is not starting from zero: it carries a
large, categorized portfolio history, 43 Story Highlights (including a
substantial trust/testimonial highlight, "اعتماد شما"), and an established
niche (website design + SEO) with a real, non-trivial existing audience.

The account's current low activity totals (524 views / 30 days, 2,084
views / 90 days) reflect **residual traffic on old content during a
dormant period**, not a verdict on content quality — there is nothing to
judge yet because nothing new has been published. The real test begins
once publishing resumes.

**Strongest existing assets:** a large, industry-diverse completed-website
portfolio; a strong trust/social-proof highlight; 12+ years of real
business history (`knowledge/company.md`); an established audience already
following for website-design/SEO content.

**Biggest revival risks:** defaulting back into the account's historical
pattern of high-volume generic SEO cards and repetitive static education;
treating a 5-item private sample as statistically conclusive; publishing
for frequency's sake rather than strategic value; locking to a visual
identity that is pending redesign.

**Highest-potential opportunities:** repositioning around Website Design
with the Website → Trust → UX → Conversion → Leads → Growth chain;
testing human/expert and conversational formats, which are historically
underused; making portfolio storytelling (not just showcase) a weekly
constant; rebuilding Story consistency as a relationship layer.

## 3. Account Inactivity Diagnosis

Zero content has been published in 90+ days, so the current private
totals are **residual activity on old content**, not a live-campaign
baseline. Low absolute numbers here are not evidence that ArasSystem's
content approach doesn't work — there is no current content to evaluate.

**What can be learned now:** the account still generates real, mostly
non-follower traffic (77–78% of views from non-followers in both windows)
even while dormant, and audience activity patterns exist (see Section 5)
that suggest people are still discoverable and reachable.

**What cannot yet be judged:** which new formats, hooks, or pillars will
perform for the *current* audience; whether historical winners (e.g. the
podcast-style outlier) would repeat; realistic reach/engagement rates for
new content; lead-generation effectiveness.

**What must be relearned after publishing resumes:** essentially
everything content-performance-related. The first 30 days exist
specifically to relearn this with real, current data (see Section 11).

## 4. Historical Content Diagnosis

| Category | Classification | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Portfolio (client websites) | **KEEP** | Large, diverse, industry-categorized asset; aligns directly with the Website Design core pillar and portfolio-as-core-priority strategy. |
| Website education | **KEEP / REFINE** | On-strategy; refine toward business-outcome framing (Website → Trust → UX → Conversion → Leads → Growth) rather than generic tips. |
| SEO education | **REDUCE** | Historically the largest content volume; strategy caps SEO at 30% subject focus, and exact SEO deliverables are still "To be defined" in `knowledge/services.md` — avoid implying unconfirmed packages. |
| Humor / relatable business content | **RETEST** | Low observed engagement in the historical sample; may still hold trust/relatability value in smaller, more targeted doses — worth retesting, not scaling back up. |
| Team / human content | **RETEST (priority)** | The one private-Insights team/behind-the-scenes Reel was the strongest performer in the small sample across every engagement metric — a clear test priority, not a proven formula. |
| Threads-style reposts | **STOP** | Off-brand repost clusters with no clear strategic goal; does not map to any content pillar or primary goal. |
| Seasonal promotions | **REDUCE** | Historical Black Friday Reels underperformed on likes/comments despite one having a strong non-follower view-share signal (per-item Reach was not separately available); use selectively and tie to a clear goal rather than volume. |
| Tech/business news | **STOP** | No clear link to ArasSystem's positioning or pillars; dilutes focus. |
| Brand / milestones | **KEEP (selective)** | Legitimate trust-building content (e.g. anniversaries) but should be occasional, not a content pillar. |

## 5. Private Insights Signals

**FACT:**
- Non-follower view share: 77.7% (30-day) and 77.6% (90-day) at the
  account level.
- Profile visits: 42 (30-day), 190 (90-day). External link taps: 1
  (30-day), 13 (90-day).
- Sampled Reels: the internal team/behind-the-scenes event Reel recorded
  2,778 views, 89 likes, 18 comments, 5 saves, 34 shares, 104 accounts
  engaged, 77.3% non-follower views — the strongest result on every
  engagement metric in the 5-item sample.
- The Black Friday "5 steps" Reel recorded 2,314 views with 91.8%
  non-follower views — the highest non-follower view share in the sampled
  Reels (not Reach, which was not separately available per-item) — but
  only 27 likes and 3 comments.
- Other sampled Reels (calligraphy, Yalda AI promo, Black Friday 20% OFF)
  had materially lower views and engagement than these two.

**INTERPRETATION:**
- High non-follower view share account-wide suggests the account is still
  being surfaced to new accounts by Instagram's discovery systems despite
  inactivity — a workable foundation for revival reach.
- The gap between the team Reel's engagement depth and the "5 steps"
  Reel's high-view-share-but-shallow-engagement pattern suggests discovery
  and engagement may respond to different content qualities.

**HYPOTHESIS (per interpretation rule — not conclusions):**
- Human / behind-the-scenes / conversational content may deserve more
  testing.
- High non-follower discovery content deserves testing as a reach lever.
- Engagement depth and discovery reach may require different formats,
  meaning a content mix may need to deliberately serve both.
- Portfolio + human expertise combined may be a valuable format to test.

This is a 5-item sample and must never be treated as statistically
conclusive (see `analytics/private/...md` Strategic Use Rule).

## 7. Major Revival Opportunities

**HIGH PRIORITY**
- Website portfolio storytelling (what/why/problem-solved, not just
  showcase)
- Website walkthrough Reels for completed projects
- Human/expert content testing (team, behind-the-scenes, expert
  commentary)
- Strategic, varied Stories rebuilding daily relationship consistency

**MEDIUM PRIORITY**
- Before/After content
- Work-in-progress / design-decision content
- Business-oriented website education (UX, conversion, credibility)
- Consultation / WhatsApp lead paths tied to relevant content

**TEST**
- Conversational/talking-head formats
- Selective SEO content tied to real audience value
- Website traffic-driving CTAs on portfolio/education content
- Social proof content frequency and format

## 8. What We Must Avoid

- Mass generic SEO card production (historical over-reliance)
- Repetitive static education with no clear goal
- Copying competitor content or formats
- Excessive promotions / seasonal-discount volume
- Empty viral content disconnected from strategy
- Constant sales/WhatsApp CTA on every piece of content
- Treating the current (pending-redesign) visual identity as permanent
- Treating historical or small-sample "winners" as mandatory templates
- Automatically changing the 70/30 Website Design/SEO default
- Publishing purely to maintain posting frequency

## 10. Data Gaps

Currently unavailable and must not be estimated:
- Reel Plays (distinct from Views), Watch time, Average watch time,
  Retention, Completion rate
- Full follower growth (net gained/lost) as a period delta; unfollows
- Audience demographics (age, gender) and location
- Per-content link-click attribution (only account-level external link
  taps exist)
- Full Story comparison set (only 1 of several archived Stories sampled)
- Lead conversion (WhatsApp/contact actions as a distinct trackable field)
- Native lifetime/longer-than-90-day account aggregate

**Closing these gaps:** every new Post, Reel, Story, and CTA published
during the 30-day plan should be tracked individually against
`analytics/instagram-insights-schema.md` so `instagram-analyst` can begin
building a real, current-data picture — closing these gaps is a direct
output of resuming publishing, not something achievable through further
historical analysis.

## 11. First 30-Day Strategic Objective

The first month is **not** about maximizing posting volume. It is about:
- Reactivating the account with consistent, quality-first publishing
- Learning what the current audience responds to
- Repositioning communication toward Website Design (with SEO as
  supporting, per the 70/30 default)
- Making portfolio visibility a weekly constant
- Testing human/expert and discovery-oriented formats as hypotheses
- Restoring Story consistency as a daily relationship layer
- Collecting the first real private performance data to replace
  historical/small-sample assumptions

See `calendar/monthly/instagram-revival-30-day-plan-v1.md` for the
operational plan built from this audit.
